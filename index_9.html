Where to cut

Scans your counted text for wordy phrases with shorter equivalents, and sentences that run long. Suggestions only — you decide what stays.

What the IB doesn’t count

Title page, contents page, headings, data tables, equations and calculations, figure/table captions, in-text citations, footnotes and endnotes used for referencing, bibliography, and appendices.

Tables made of sentences — control-variables or evaluation tables — are counted by default here, since prose in a table is still prose. Untick “written tables” if your school treats them differently.

Quotations do count, along with your introduction, body and conclusion.

Heads-up: examiners stop reading at the word limit, and substantive analysis hidden in footnotes or appendices is ignored — don’t use exclusions to smuggle in extra argument. Auto-detection is a helper, not a guarantee; skim the greyed-out parts to check they’re right.
